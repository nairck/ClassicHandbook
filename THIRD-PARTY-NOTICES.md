## Data

Game data comes from [OSMS Data Explorer](https://osmsdataexplorer.com) (CC BY 4.0); drop rates measured in Classic
World from [MapleClassic Wiki](https://mapleclassic.wiki)'s kill tests (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); other drop rates from
[osmlib.com](https://osmlib.com) and [MapleStory Quest](https://maplestory.quest); prices from public market sites,
each credited in the app. Classic Handbook's own shared data is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (database/README.md).

MapleStory and its names and art are Nexon's. Classic Handbook is an unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Nexon.
