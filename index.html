Welcome to the Classic Handbook live tracker

Use it at your own risk: neither the program nor its creator is responsible for your account.

  • The tracker works from screenshots only. You share the MapleStory window with this page (your browser asks which window), the way Discord or a browser shares a window. You choose how often, from every 5 seconds to every 10 minutes, each at a random moment.
  • Nexon's Terms of Service (Sept 7, 2026) and Code of Conduct (V) prohibit bots, macros, data mining and software that modifies the game. The tracker does none of these: it never reads or changes the game or sends it any input. Neither document mentions screenshots.
  • No bans have been reported in global MapleStory or Classic World for a screenshot-only tool. One Classic World player got a Lie Detector test on starting an overlay that captured every 1.5 seconds (Oct 2026). In Mapleland (Korea), about 8 players got 1-day suspensions (Dec 2025 to Jun 2026) with a tracker that captured every second on a fixed clock. This tracker captures far less often, at random moments.

Nexon Terms of Service · Mapleland ban report · Classic World Lie Detector report

What it does to each screenshot

  1. Finds the game. Finds the MapleStory window by its title.
  2. Takes one screenshot. One picture of that window, at a random time.
  3. Locates each window. Trained networks find the status bar, minimap, inventory, message stream and party list, and the stat, skill and Party windows when they're open.
  4. Reads the numbers. The same networks read them, here on your PC.
  5. Deletes the screenshot. Deleted at once: never saved, sent or kept. Only the numbers stay.

How often a screenshot is taken

Drag the two handles to set the shortest and longest wait between screenshots. Each wait is drawn at random from the bell between them, so reads never fall on a regular beat.

You need the desktop app to use the live tracker. It reads your game window on your PC, which a web page can't do. The desktop app is coming soon. Screenshots still work on this page: Get info from screenshot, Upload or Ctrl+V, or a screenshot dropped on the page, for your stats, your party and your inventory.
