Welcome to the Classic Handbook live tracker

Use it at your own risk: the program and its creator take no responsibility for your account.

  • Nexon's Terms of Service (updated Sept 7, 2026) say nothing about taking or reading screenshots. Their Code of Conduct (section V) bans bots, macros, data mining and third-party software that modifies the game.
  • No ban is confirmed in global MapleStory or Classic World for a tool that only reads screenshots. In Mapleland (Korea), about 8 players reported 1-day "external program" bans from Dec 2025 to Jun 2026 while running an EXP tracker that read the screen every second (1 Hz).
  • This tracker reads at most once every 5 s (0.2 Hz), at random times, and never touches the game: no memory reads, no keys, no clicks.

What it does to each screenshot

  1. Finds the game. Looks for the MapleStory window by its title. Nothing is sent to it.
  2. Takes one screenshot. One picture of that window, at a random time in your read range.
  3. Picks out the parts. The status bar (level, class, HP, MP, EXP), minimap, inventory, and the EXP, party bonus, mesos and drops messages; with Auto update on, the stat, skill and Party windows, found wherever they're open.
  4. Reads the numbers. Turned into text on your PC.
  5. Deletes the screenshot. Read in memory and deleted at once: never saved, sent or kept. Only the numbers stay, averaged over your Smooth setting.

How often it reads

Drag the two handles to set the shortest and longest wait between screenshots. Each wait is drawn at random from the bell between them, so reads never fall on a regular beat.

You need the desktop app to use the live tracker. It reads your game window on your PC, which a web page can't do. Download Classic Handbook from github.com/nairck/ClassicHandbook-source (ClassicHandbook.exe, or run.py with Python) and open the Live tracker tab there. Screenshots still work on this page: Get info from screenshot, Upload or Ctrl+V, or a screenshot dropped on the page, for your stats, your party and your inventory.
